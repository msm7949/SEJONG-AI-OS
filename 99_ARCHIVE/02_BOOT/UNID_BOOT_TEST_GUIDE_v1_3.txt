=========================

Purpose
Test whether different AI systems understand the SEJONG-AI-OS philosophy and system structure.

Steps
1. Open a new AI session
2. Paste SEJONG-AI-OS BOOT TEMPLATE
3. Ask the AI:

   - What is the SEJONG-AI-OS system?
   - What are the core principles?
   - Why are AI, Human, Record, and Collaboration included?
   - Why are constitutional values and culture included?

Success Criteria
- AI understands SEJONG-AI-OS as a human-centered AI collaboration system
- AI recognizes the importance of Record and Verification
- AI identifies philosophy, culture, and constitutional values as guidance
