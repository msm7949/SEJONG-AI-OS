SEJONG-AI-OS RECORD SCHEMA v2

Purpose
Extend SEJONG-AI-OS records with authorship, summarization, and handoff fields.

CORE FIELDS
record_SEJONG-AI-OS
record_type
topic
question
participants
responses
summary
decision
human_verify
timestamp
tags
archive_level
source_reference

NEW FIELDS
author
summary_writer
handoff_sender
handoff_receiver
ai_namespace
related_ai_records
active_context_id

FIELD MEANING
author
- who created the record

summary_writer
- who wrote the summary

handoff_sender
- who passed the record onward

handoff_receiver
- target AI or human reviewer

ai_namespace
- record ownership scope
  Example: SEJONG-AI-OS_RECORD_GPT

related_ai_records
- linked AI channels
  Example: SEJONG-AI-OS_RECORD_GEMINI, SEJONG-AI-OS_RECORD_VERIFY

active_context_id
- current task / thread continuity id

EXAMPLE
record_SEJONG-AI-OS: SEJONG-AI-OS-RECORD-0042
author: GPT
summary_writer: GPT
handoff_sender: GPT
handoff_receiver: Gemini
ai_namespace: SEJONG-AI-OS_RECORD_GPT
related_ai_records: SEJONG-AI-OS_RECORD_GEMINI, SEJONG-AI-OS_FORUM_RECORD
